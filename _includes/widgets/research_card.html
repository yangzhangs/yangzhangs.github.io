Research

Modern software is produced as artifacts, assembled through pipelines, and sustained by ecosystems. We study this continuum to make software engineering more intelligent, reliable, and sustainable.

Software Artifacts

Dockerfiles, code examples, and issue reports — the artifacts developers produce. We characterize their quality and executability, and build intelligent techniques to generate and repair them.

Software Pipelines

Container builds, CI/CD workflows, cloud-native deployment, and MLOps — the pipelines that deliver software. We diagnose faults, predict performance, and improve reliability and efficiency.
